## 12 Practical Use Cases

These twelve use cases represent **only the starting point**; because the engine is built on **standard web foundations**, any structure you can **map onto a folder tree** can be deployed here - feel free to **experiment and propose** your own.

In Short: Personal Subcreative Archives, Federated Project Hubs, Distraction-Free Courseware, Intentional Community Portals, High-Integrity Business Websites, Decentralized Media Libraries, Interactive Slide & Presentation Hubs, Open-Access Knowledge Bases, Private Team Workspaces, Non-Profit & Mission Portals, Historical & Legacy Vaults, Modular Multi-Site Webrings<!--more-->

1. **Personal Subcreative Archives:** A quiet, permanent digital home for writers, poets, and thinkers to organize lifelong essays, creative works, and personal journals in human-readable Markdown without database reliance.
1. **Federated Project Hubs:** Clean, structured websites for small teams, research groups, or specialized collectives needing clear project documentation, shared goals, and transparent public records.
1. **Distraction-Free Courseware:** Light, responsive platforms for educators and independent mentors to host structured learning pathways (like the Ganymede framework) without intrusive tracking or heavy LMS software.
1. **Intentional Community Portals:** Digital anchors for local or remote intentional communities, providing shared calendars, mission statements, and transparent charters ("Who Is" documentation).
1. **High-Integrity Business Websites:** Sleek, low-maintenance web presence for ethical businesses, consultants, and independent craftsmen who require absolute reliability without security overhead.
1. **Decentralized Media Libraries:** Sovereign media repositories utilizing lean subdomains to stream audio, showcase photography, or host long-form audio-visual projects without algorithmic gatekeeping.
1. **Interactive Slide & Presentation Hubs:** Integrated web presentations leveraging slide engines like RevealJS directly within your folder hierarchy for dynamic, web-native decks.
1. **Open-Access Knowledge Bases:** Interconnected reference libraries and curated bibliographies designed for fast, offline-first access and long-term digital preservation.
1. **Private Team Workspaces:** Lightweight internal knowledge hubs for development or operational teams needing clear standards, code documentation, and task break-down structures.
1. **Non-Profit & Mission Portals:** Transparent, non-extractive sites for social initiatives requiring clear public records, donor clarity, and structured, user-initiated contact flows.
1. **Historical & Legacy Vaults:** Digital preservation of historical papers, family archives, or institutional records stored in raw format to ensure accessibility across decades.
1. **Modular Multi-Site Webrings:** Interconnected clusters of domain-specific sites sharing a unified styling canvas while maintaining total content independence across subdomains.
