## 5 Long-Term Alignment Criteria

The future web must allow **collaboration with sincerity**, shared purpose, and **reverence for human dignity**.

Socially and divinely centered web asks not only for **elegant technology**, but for relationships built on **intention, transparency, mutual care**, and collective wisdom.

When a digital platform is designed to support **meaningful contribution** rather than extraction, it becomes a space for **cooperation, spiritual alignment, and humane governance** across distributed teams and networks.<!--more-->

* **Purity of Intention:** Alignment with DAWN requires a genuine commitment to constructive, non-extractive goals. We actively filter out projects driven purely by short-term speculation, rage-farming, or manipulative monetization.
* **Commitment to Sovereignty:** Users must value long-term ownership of their data. We align best with individuals and teams willing to embrace basic version control (Git) and offline-first workflows to guarantee their own digital independence.
* **Radical Transparency:** Operating within our federated network means embracing open governance. Top-level charters and reasonings for partnership must be clearly stated, fostering trust across the network.
* **Rejection of Algorithmic Noise:** We partner with creators who prioritize depth, clarity, and genuine human engagement over clickbait, growth hacking, and automated engagement loops.
* **Respect for Mutual Effort:** We operate on mutual respect, transparent valuation of time, and shared abundance. Those who join the ecosystem recognize the labor of the architects and mentors showing them the ropes, maintaining a healthy, sustainable balance of value exchange.
