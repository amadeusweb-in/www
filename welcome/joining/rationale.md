**1. On Handling Disagreement and Exit Protocols**

* **Question:** If you reach a fundamental disagreement with the core direction or leadership of the network, how will you document your departure?
* **Context:** Modern digital spaces often default to public ghosting, aggressive call-outs, or passive-aggressive sabotage when friction arises. DAWN requires upfront terms of engagement for both joining and parting ways.
* **Importance:** Assesses whether the candidate possesses the emotional maturity to handle ideological friction with dignity, public record transparency, and mutual respect rather than spite.

---

**2. On Navigating Unearned Entitlement**

* **Question:** How do you react when a community or network sets hard boundaries against your immediate expectations or personal desires?
* **Context:** Entitlement frequently manifests as demands for instant accommodation, unearned access, or breaking established community rules under the guise of personal uniqueness.
* **Importance:** Tests if the applicant can respect system-level boundaries without taking operational enforcement as a personal insult or existential attack.

---

**3. On the Reality of Financial and Labor Valuation**

* **Question:** What is your reaction when asked to compensate an architect or mentor directly for their time and technical labor?
* **Context:** The modern web has conditioned users to expect "free" platforms, hiding the true cost behind data extraction, advertising, and uncompensated labor. DAWN explicitly honors the shepherd and charges for time and infrastructure.
* **Importance:** Evaluates whether the candidate respects fair economic exchange and non-extractive sustainability, or harbors an extractive, consumerist mindset toward labor.

---

**4. On Navigating Egocentric Impulses**

* **Question:** When your creative work or technical proposal is critiqued for bloat or lack of alignment, how do you manage your ego?
* **Context:** High-intent creative and technical environments require continuous deconstruction. Individuals attached to personal vanity often interpret structural pruning as a personal rejection.
* **Importance:** Measures the applicant's capacity for constructive deconstruction and their ability to separate personal identity from project output.

**5. On Pluralism versus Dogmatism**

* **Question:** How do you engage with an atheist or skeptic who rigorously challenges your deeply held spiritual or philosophical convictions?
* **Context:** DAWN deliberately welcomes agnostics and skeptics as essential "checks and balances" to prevent ideological decay and spiritual dogma.
* **Importance:** Identifies whether the candidate can coexist in a pluralistic, logic-tested environment without devolving into religious or philosophical self-righteousness.

**6. On the Nature of Long-Term Commitment**

* **Question:** How do you maintain focus on a three-year content or architectural outline when immediate validation and dopamine metrics are absent?
* **Context:** The modern internet conditions people for instant engagement metrics, likes, and rapid algorithmic feedback. DAWN prioritizes slow, iterative, multi-year subcreation.
* **Importance:** Determines if the candidate possesses the internal discipline required for long-term construction versus short-term attention-seeking.

### 5 Stipulations by the Founder & Leadership

* **Stipulation 1: Absolute Data Autonomy.** The founder and network leads will never claim ownership over an individual's intellectual property, raw text, or subcreative archives; all content hosted on local Git repositories remains 100% sovereign to the creator.
* **Stipulation 2: Non-Interference in Core Architecture.** Members must accept that the fundamental codebase (AmadeusWeb Spring) and server hosting architecture are strictly managed by the founder to preserve the zero-SQL, zero-bloat security model.
* **Stipulation 3: Mandatory "Who Is" Transparency.** Every member or team hosting a federated node must maintain a live, publicly accessible "Who Is" folder detailing their core pledges, project goals, and terms of engagement.
* **Stipulation 4: Direct Financial Integrity.** Platform fees, hosting costs, and architectural mentoring time are non-negotiable direct transactions; no hidden monetization, ads, or third-party data trading will ever be introduced to subsidize costs.
* **Stipulation 5: Firm Boundary Enforcement.** Leadership reserves the right to suspend or expel any participant who repeatedly breaches self-made promises, exploits others, or introduces toxic pettiness, with the full context made part of the public record.

### 5 Leader Guidelines for Applicant Counter-Questions

* **Guideline 1: Assess Long-Term Architectural Continuity.** Encouraging applicants to ask how the network guarantees uptime, backup integrity, and long-term hosting sustainability if the core founder steps back.
* **Guideline 2: Evaluate the Real Cost of Mentorship.** Welcoming direct questions regarding the exact time commitment, financial expectations, and hands-on support provided during the onboarding and training phase.
* **Guideline 3: Probe the Limits of Creative Flexibility.** Guiding applicants to ask specific technical questions about how their specific design, layout, or media needs fit within the constraints of the flat-file framework.
* **Guideline 4: Clarify Governance and Dispute Resolution.** Supporting applicants in asking how disputes between network members are mediated and what specific checks exist to prevent arbitrary leadership decisions.
* **Guideline 5: Interrogate Mission Alignment.** Welcoming inquiries about how the network actively routes traffic or builds webring connections between federated sites without using invasive tracking or centralized algorithms.
